## A. static/index.html

**A1. Autosave "restore offer" block — flow does not exist.**
- Location: lines 143–147, `#autosaveRestore`: "Unsaved work from a previous visit was found." / "Restore it" / "Discard".
- PROBLEM: stale v8 flow. v9 autosaves to localStorage and **auto-loads silently at boot** (`state.ts loadState()`); nothing in `app.ts` ever unhides this block or wires `#autosaveRestoreBtn` / `#autosaveDiscardBtn`. Dead UI that contradicts the help modal's (correct) "autosaves to your browser".
- PROPOSED: delete the block. (If a restore offer is ever wanted, build the flow first.)

**A2. Hero subhead promises "live EVE market prices" the planner can't currently deliver.**
- Location: line 211: "…the planner works out the most profitable thing you can actually build with what you've got, using live EVE market prices…".
- PROBLEM: the planner's price fetch is user-triggered (a "Fetch live Jita prices (ESI)" button), and in this build the ID registry is PARTIAL — only 4 commodities (Aqueous Liquids, Water, Coolant, Broadcast Node) have type IDs (`generated-ids.ts`), so almost everything comes back UNPRICED and must be typed in. The reference panels do show live prices for all 101, but the *planner* mostly runs on manual quotes today.
- PROPOSED: "…using Jita market prices — fetched live where this build can, entered by you where it can't — and the game's real physical limits."

**A3. Quickstart Section 3 line — same live-price overclaim.**
- Location: line 219: "Taxes, fees, freight, and live Jita prices — including whether you sell instantly or list orders…".
- PROBLEM: as A2 — "live Jita prices" reads as automatic and complete.
- PROPOSED: "Taxes, fees, freight, and Jita prices (fetch what's available live, type in the rest) — including whether you sell instantly or list orders…".

**A4. Market Reference sub promises hover *highlighting* that was removed.**
- Location: line 344: "Hover one to highlight every planet in your plan that touches it."
- PROBLEM: v9 removed the highlight half of this feature (`19-market-grid.js` comment: the generated-console tiles it highlighted are gone). Hover now shows a **popup listing** your planets.
- PROPOSED: "Hover one to see which of your planets touch it."

**A5. Market Reference sub claims the planner can plan composites — it can't in v9.**
- Location: line 344: "**Built from PI** are products the planner can plan for you — fuel blocks, nanite paste and deployables. **Bought inputs** are the non-PI materials those need… the planner prices them as a cost and charges freight on them, never treating them as free."
- PROBLEM: stale v8 claim. The v9 product dropdown is `SCHEMATICS.keys()` (`src/spec/schematics.ts`) — PI P1–P4 only. No fuel block, nanite paste, or deployable can be selected, so the planner never plans them nor prices their non-PI inputs. In v9 these two columns are reference/price-lookup only.
- PROPOSED: "**Built from PI** are things made from PI — fuel blocks, nanite paste and deployables — shown with live prices for reference. **Bought inputs** are the non-PI materials those recipes need. (This build's planner plans the PI chain itself, P1–P4.)"

**A6. Templates sub: "Every template the tool can generate" — most are not generated.**
- Location: line 405: "Every template the tool can generate. Copy one, then in EVE open the planet…"
- PROBLEM: 199 of the 259 listed templates are community files stored verbatim (`20b-imported-templates.js`); only 60 (15 miner + 24 P2 + 21 P3) are generated by the tool. The group notes themselves say so.
- PROPOSED: "Every template in the library — the layouts this tool generates plus 199 community templates, copied exactly and credited to their authors. Copy one, then in EVE open the planet, click **Import** and paste."

**A7. Footer legal line attributes EVE to "Fenris Creations (formerly CCP Games)".**
- Location: line 562: "EVE Online and all related marks are the property of Fenris Creations (formerly CCP Games)."
- PROBLEM: unsupported. Every source in the verified library (CCP dev docs, CCP Rubicon blog, support.eveonline.com, 2026-dated citations) attributes EVE to CCP; no rename to "Fenris Creations" appears anywhere in the research. A wrong legal attribution is worse than none.
- PROPOSED: "EVE Online and all related marks are the property of CCP hf."

**A8. Quickstart implies program length is per-character.**
- Location: line 217: "**Section 1 — Your Operation.** Each character individually: planets (max 6…), skills, and your **extraction program length**…"
- PROBLEM: in v9 program length is one operation-wide setting (`state.programHours`), not per character.
- PROPOSED: "…Each character individually: planets (max 6 with Interplanetary Consolidation V) and skills — plus one **extraction program length** for the whole operation…"

**A9. Price History sub says "highs and lows" are shown; the chart plots daily averages.**
- Location: lines 361–362: "Daily Jita highs and lows straight from public ESI market history. All 101 items are plotted at once…"
- PROBLEM: `22-price-history.js draw()` plots `pt.avg` only; highs/lows merely set the axis range.
- PROPOSED: "Daily Jita average prices straight from public ESI market history. All 101 items are plotted at once…"

**A10. Help modal step 3 — "load live prices" (same caveat as A2).**
- Location: line 453: "Enter your costs — tax, fees, shipping — and load live prices."
- PROBLEM: as A2/A3; also every price a fetch can't resolve must be entered manually.
- PROPOSED: "Enter your costs — tax, fees, shipping — and load or enter Jita prices."

**A11. Help modal calls mode 1 "most profit"; the engine maximizes output of the chosen product.**
- Location: lines 496–499: "Four ways to define 'best': **most profit**, **a quota**…"
- PROBLEM: minor honesty gap. The "max" mode runs `solveMax` — maximum *output* of the product you picked, priced afterwards; it does not search across products for profit (that is "compare everything"), and if the product's per-unit net is negative it maximizes the loss and reports it. The Section 4 label ("Maximum output/profit from my planets") has the same ambiguity.
- PROPOSED: "…**most output of your chosen product** (then priced honestly — a loss shows as a loss), **a quota**, **a login budget**, or **compare everything** (the profit ranking across every product)."

## B. src/ui/app.ts

**B1. Character table column header "Budget".**
- Location: line 144: headers `…'Broker Relations', 'Budget', ''` over a cell rendering "N planets".
- PROBLEM: reads as an ISK budget; it is the character's planet allowance (1 + IC level).
- PROPOSED: header "Planet budget" (or "Planets").

**B2. Fetch-failure note surfaces a developer instruction to end users.**
- Location: line 290 (priceNote) relaying `ids.ts` errors: `UNPRICED: Water (missing-typeid: "Water" not in the generated registry (partial) — run tools/gen-sde.mjs)`.
- PROBLEM: accurate for a developer, meaningless on the website — a visitor cannot run `tools/gen-sde.mjs`. (Error text lives in `src/data/ids.ts:28`.)
- PROPOSED (UI-side rewording when displayed): "no type ID for Water in this build — enter its quote manually."

**B3. Compare mode truncates silently.**
- Location: lines 512 and 518–519: summary says "N viable products ranked" but the table renders `ranked.slice(0, 15)` and the excluded list `slice(0, 40)` with no "showing top…" note.
- PROBLEM: when more than 15 rank (or 40 are excluded), the page under-reports without saying so — against the site's own "never silently" voice.
- PROPOSED: caption the table "top 15 shown of N" (and "first 40 shown" on the excluded fold) when truncated.

**B4. Mode label ambiguity (pairs with A11).**
- Location: line 363: `['max', 'Maximum output/profit from my planets']`.
- PROPOSED: "Maximum output of my chosen product".
