How to use Solving PI

This tool tells you what to build on your planets and how much ISK you will make. You type in what you have; it does the math. It never asks for your EVE login, and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

Get your plan — four steps

  1. Pick a goal in section 1. Not sure what to make? Choose Compare — it finds the money for you.
  2. Add what you have in section 2: your characters (one press creates a maxed roster — fix any skills that aren’t, then ✓ Done), and your planets — press I know my system to type its name, or Find me a home to scout a region.
  3. Tap where you operate in section 3 (High/Low/Null/Wormhole). Live Jita prices load and refresh themselves.
  4. Press the gold SOLVE button at the bottom. Your plan appears in section 4.

Never feel lost: the bar at the bottom names your next step, each section shows ✓ when done, and every open section has a Next → button. If SOLVE is grey, read the bar.

Simple and Advanced

The site starts Simple: smart defaults handle accuracy, sourcing and costs. The Advanced switch (top right) reveals every dial — the accuracy level, sourcing pins, the blend builder, flat-density tools, the full price and cost tables, program length, and deep analytics. Nothing changes underneath; you just see more knobs.

The five goals

  • Compare — “what should I make?” Ranks everything by profit. Press Plan this → on a row for its full plan.
  • Fit my logins — the best money for how often you can log in.
  • Max output — as much as possible of one thing you choose.
  • Pick for me — hands-free. Picks the product and the plan for you.
  • Weekly target — make a set amount each week.

Where parts come from

Every part can be mined, refined from bought ore, or bought ready-made — even the middle parts (buy P3s, run just the P4 factory). The tool decides by default and tells you why. To choose yourself, use ⚙ Adjust where parts come from on your results, or Advanced’s sourcing panel in section 1. Anything you set is never changed.

Making more than one thing

Press + Add a second product on your results (or the blend builder in Advanced) and give each product a share. Shares always add up to 100.

How exact are the numbers?

Automatic. No scans yet → quick estimates from your space type, clearly labeled. Real scans → your numbers. Costs confirmed → nothing assumed at all. (Advanced can force a level.)

Your planets

Each planet shows its resources as chips — click one (or ✎ Edit) to enter your scan’s density %. New planets start at 70% until you do; over 100% is real. Tick Complete to fold a finished planet away — each system header has a Complete & Collapse All.

Region Scout — where should I live?

In section 2, press Find me a home, pick a region, and it ranks every system in it for your goal. The traffic tag shows how dangerous each system was in the last hour. Press Load planets → on a winner and they drop into your planner. Scout numbers are estimates until you scan.

Reading your plan

The answer comes first — one card with your ISK per week — then three tabs: Plan (what to build), Money (the ledger), Why (what’s limiting you and what was chosen for you).

The plan shows: your money per week (a loss shows as a loss — never hidden), what each character builds (press Copy template to paste a colony into the game — ⚠︎ means double-check it first), what’s slowing you down, and why each choice was made. Numbers built on guesses carry an amber ESTIMATE label.

If your goal can’t be done, the tool says why in plain words — often with a one-click fix, like Set target to N/wk.

Saving

Everything saves in your browser as you type. Save My Data downloads a backup file you can load anywhere.

Good to know

  • No login, no passwords — public data only.
  • Prices move. Solve again before spending big.
  • It plans; you build. It can’t click the in-game buttons for you.

The reference sections

Press REFERENCE (below Results) to switch to them; PLANNER brings the steps back.

  • All PI Visualized — any commodity’s whole chain, drawn.
  • Market Reference — live Jita prices for every PI item.
  • Price History — the same prices, charted over time.
  • PI Templates — ready-made colony layouts. Copy, paste in game, done.
  • System Status — is EVE up right now?

If something looks wrong

Press ⟲ Reset on the section that’s acting up, press Refresh now in section 3, and solve again. Still wrong? Press Contact / Report a Bug and tell us what happened.
